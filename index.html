01Guided fixes

Walkthroughs that explain the why.

Pick your symptom, run the diagnostics, move forward one step at a time:

  • Can't see Dante devices
  • Clock sync problems
  • Routing & audio dropouts
  • DVS & recording rigs
  • Redundant networks sharing one switch
  • Firmware panics & stuck updates
02Real diagnostics

Not a checklist PDF.

The app actually talks to your network:

  • Full subnet scan in ~5 seconds — IP, MAC, vendor, hostname
  • mDNS discovery scan, same mechanism as Dante Controller
  • Isolation check for Wi-Fi, VPNs, dual-homed machines
  • Ping & UDP port probes with honest results
  • Subnet comparator — can these two IPs even see each other?
03Field knowledge

Brand quirks, baked in.

The manufacturer gotchas that never make it into the manual:

  • Yamaha CL/QL clocking defaults & Console IDs
  • Allen & Heath clock-chain rules & static IP traps
  • DiGiCo DMI counts at 96k, Orange Box hierarchy
  • d&B DS100 failsafe LEDs — why amps never appear
  • NEXO NXDT104 card requirement
04Escalation

Reports that make you look organized.

Every diagnostic gets logged with timestamps. When you finally have to call someone, hit Copy Report and paste the whole session into your ticket.

Vendor support will think you're organized. You're not. You just have this app.

Install.

Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 12 or later.

Grab the DMG — the Download button up top always has the latest version.

Unzip and drag Dante Troubleshooter.app to Applications.

First launch: macOS will complain we're unsigned — like all the best touring crews. Right-click → Open → Open, or approve via System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. One time only.

Plug into the network and pick your symptom. Doors are in 40 minutes. You've got this.
